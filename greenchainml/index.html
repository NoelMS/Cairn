Risk: planning under uncertainty

Two-stage stochastic model: supplier contracts and reserved tonnages are fixed before 20 sampled futures (regional demand swings, supplier outages, Red Sea and Hormuz closures); tonnages and modes then adapt in each. The robust plan is compared with a plan made for average demand. Unserved demand is charged at ten times the product price, so the gap mostly measures avoided shortfalls.

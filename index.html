Methods in play

Integrated model
Supplier selection, order allocation and transport mode choice as a mixed-integer program; an epsilon-constraint front of cost against CO2; TOPSIS picks the balanced plan
Scenarios and risk
The lab re-solves the model for disruptions, carbon prices and demand shifts; a two-stage stochastic model prices robustness (VSS, CVaR)
Supplier ranking
TOPSIS on cost, CO2e, transit time and social score; AHP pairwise weighting in the suite
Emission factors
DESNZ 2024 well-to-wheel freight factors for road, rail and sea; electric trucks use the grid intensity of the country they run in
Vehicle routing
Depot choice and fleet mix in three regions, scored against every feasible plan and its Pareto front
Three pillars
Economic, environmental and social scores in the model, the lab, the routing exercise and the suite

    How to use the Scenario lab

    1

    Start from the baseline

    The lab first solves today's network: 21 real manufacturers supplying modules, steel, aluminium, copper cable and turbines to 19 real solar and wind projects. Each solve builds the whole cost and CO2e trade-off front.

    2

    Change the world

    Pick a preset or change anything yourself: close a sea lane, switch suppliers or transport modes off, scale demand by region or add a carbon price. Then press Solve.

    3

    Read what changed

    The chart shows the new front over the baseline in grey. Compare the cheapest plan, which is what a market buyer would pick and what a carbon price acts on, or the balanced plan chosen by TOPSIS with your weights. The list explains which suppliers, tonnages and modes moved.

    4

    Plan under uncertainty and compare

    Turn on Plan under uncertainty to fix contracts before demand and disruptions are known, and see what that robustness is worth. Save scenarios to compare them side by side, or export the allocation as CSV.

        How to play Fleet routing

        1

        Set your weights

        Decide how much cost, carbon and community impact matter to you. Community impact is diesel truck-km driven through residential areas. The score compares your plan with the best plan for these weights, so the same routes can score differently for different priorities.

        2

        Pick a region, then open depots

        Choose the UAE, the Benelux North Sea coast or the Yangtze River Delta. Each has two depots with different daily costs and positions; in the UAE, Jebel Ali is close to the Abu Dhabi bound sites but costs more, while Hamriyah is cheaper but sits in a residential area. You pay for every depot you open.

        3

        Route the trucks

        Pick a truck, choose its depot, then click sites on the chart or in the list, in delivery order. Diesel trucks carry more and cost less; electric trucks emit far less, count zero residential diesel km, but have a 250 km range. A truck that breaks capacity, range or the 10 hour shift is flagged in red.

        4

        Score against the optimizer

        Once every site is on a feasible route, score the plan. The optimizer enumerates every feasible plan, finds the Pareto-optimal ones and picks the best for your weights. You can then overlay its routes on the chart.
